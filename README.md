# Spotique

_"Private spots. Public city."_

Spotique is an hourly private-parking marketplace. It connects residents with underutilized driveways and garages to drivers who need affordable, convenient parking — starting in Jackson Heights, Queens (zip 11372), where commercial streets are busy but commercial parking is scarce.

## How it works

- **Hosts** list a driveway or garage with photos, an hourly rate, and a weekly availability schedule
- **Drivers** browse spots on a map, filter by date and time, and request a booking
- **Hosts** accept or decline; on accept, the full address and host contact are revealed to the driver
- **Payment** happens off-platform in cash, Venmo, or Zelle on arrival — no in-app payments in MVP
- **Both parties** rate each other after the booking, building trust over time

## MVP scope

- Free for hosts and drivers during the pilot
- Jackson Heights only (expansion comes after product-market fit)
- Hourly bookings only (no monthly/long-term)
- No in-app payments, no in-app chat
